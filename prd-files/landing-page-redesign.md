### Feature of Pitch Like This

Following is a list of features, each feature should get a website section with title and description along with an interactive coded asset that describes the feature within the section.

Use the same text copy as written below and add placeholder copy when not available.

1. Document Case Studies

    Title: "Document Stories from your Career"
    Description: "Document your experiences as case studies, quantify your impacts, and link them to live prototypes, designs, or repositories"

    Interactive Asset: Create a Stack of Cards which are individual Case Studies, the user can interact by clicking on left and right end of this stack to rotate the cards.

    Each card is a Story or Case Study. Design the card in a landscape rectangular frame with name of the role at the top right corner. A very short title of the experience and brief description. At the bottom of the card mention quantitatives with icons for each metric.

2. Create Campaigns

    Title: "Create Campaigns and Share them as Pitches"
    Description: "Create campaigns from your Stories and Pitch those campaigns to hiring managers, founders, or decision makers with a one-time shareable link."

3. Track the Impact of your Campaigns 

    Title: Track the Impact of your Campaigns 
    Description: Keep track of visitors on your campaigns, their actions, and time spent. Use this insight to create campaigns that convert.

4. Collect Leads for your Camapaigns

    Title: Collect Leads and Reach Out to them later 
    Description: Hiring managers or decision makers impressed by your campaigns can share their contact details. Filter reach out and close the opportunities.

5. Organise Campaigns within Projects

    Title: Update Shared Pitches without resharing links
    Description: Switch campaigns that you want to be Active and available on your shared pitch links. Each project can have one active and many inactive campaigns. Shared links auto update your active campaign.

### Is This for Me
This section of the website helps the user identify themselves as a user type, on clicking a User Type listed below, they can view the use cases of that particular user type.

1. Those looking for Jobs: Everyone is sending cold emails, we help you level up with more interactive pitches. Resumes are too generic - we give you tools to make a personalized pitch. Job Boards do not help you convert, our goal is to give you tools that convert opportunities.

2. Those looking for Clients: These are Freelancers who develop technical products for their clients. Make personalized pitches to your clients, showcase contextual projects they will relate to. Convert prospects to warm leads and reach out to them once they have interacted to your campaigns. Analyse Active Sessions, Engaged Sessions, and Time Spent - use insights to make decisions.


3. Miscelleneous or Founder: These can be people pitching ideas to investors via this tool or hiring managers pitches job roles to potential candidates. Spend more time framing your pitch and showcasing correct details over figuring out ways to pitch. Perfect your pitches on the go - modify anytime without have to reshare a new link. A fully secure system where only you can access your leads - archieve campaigns to remove them from public view.
